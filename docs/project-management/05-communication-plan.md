## 1. Purpose

This plan sets out who gets which project information, how often, through which channel, and who sends it. The team is ten people working in overlapping streams, so the plan keeps updates short and keeps one place for the truth about tasks.

## 2. Principles

- **ClickUp is the source of truth** for tasks, owners, dates and status.
- **Decisions are written down** in the [Change Control Log](06-change-control-log.md) or the [Risk Register](04-risk-register.md). Chat is for coordination.
- **Client communication goes through the Team Lead** at the scheduled presentations.
- **Bad news travels early.** Anything that threatens a presentation date, a gate or the release is escalated the same day.

## 5. Escalation Rules

| Level | Trigger | Escalate To | Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Task blocked for more than one working day | Associate Project Manager | Same day |
| 2 | A presentation date, phase gate or release is at risk | Team Lead | Within one working day |
| 3 | A change that affects scope, schedule or the release date | Team Lead decides through change control | Before work continues on the item |
